What it is like here

Brisbane specifically

Brisbane road and rail projects run constantly and traffic control is one of the fastest routes into decent money without a certificate.

Before you enrol

Three things worth asking

  • Ask which tickets they cover, because you usually need more than one.
  • Ask whether they place you with a company afterwards.
  • Ask about night rates, since that is where the money is.
